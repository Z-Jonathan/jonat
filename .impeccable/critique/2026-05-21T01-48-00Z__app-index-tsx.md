### Anti-Patterns Verdict

Passes every absolute ban. Passes the first-order reflex check (no neon-on-dark deals-app cliche). Fails the second-order reflex check: "deals-app-that-isn't-Groupon -> dark slate + one warm amber accent on the urgency word" is itself a saturated 2026 consumer-marketplace lane (Uber Eats / DoorDash / Resy promo aesthetic).

### Overall Impression

Technically excellent, strategically wrong. The craft (h-14 CTAs, justify-between composition, thumb-zone gravity, hitSlop, header role, active-state language) is right. The voice that craft is in service of is what PRODUCT.md just told us not to ship.

Biggest opportunity: rewrite the brand voice on this screen to match the discovery + playful + anti-Groupon brief, and let the existing craft carry the new voice. Layout doesn't move. What the layout is saying changes.

### What's Working

1. The proof line is the brand in microcosm. "Croissants this morning. Pints at five. Tacos at ten." nails Principle 2 (friend, not marketer) and Principle 4 (show the thing). Best line on the screen.
2. Composition rhythm. justify-between with title-top / CTA-bottom is the right shape, well-paced, thumb-native.
3. CTA system cohesion. h-14, active-state language, secondary border treatment tie cleanly into login/signup.

### Priority Issues

**[P0] Urgency palette promoted to brand voice (Principle 3 violation).**
- *What:* text-soon (the #f59e0b amber that flags "expires within 24h" inside the product) is the brand accent on the DIBS kicker and the word *gone.* PRODUCT.md: "They are not the brand accent. Don't promote them to the brand voice on landing/marketing surfaces; doing so collapses Dibs into the Groupon lane."
- *Why it matters:* Exact anti-reference. Maya (Groupon-skeptic) bounces. Users learn the wrong vocabulary before they even open the product.
- *Fix:* Drop text-soon from the landing. Possibly no accent at all on this surface.
- *Command:* /impeccable colorize app/index.tsx (or bundle into /impeccable bolder).

**[P1] Tagline is urgency-framed when brand is discovery-framed (Principle 1 violation).**
- *What:* "Local deals, before they're gone." Reads as hurry; user picked Discovery.
- *Why it matters:* Wrong first contract. Sam (discovery-seeker, primary persona) wanted to wander.
- *Fix:* Rewrite toward find/wander/neighborhood ("The good stuff your block is up to" / "Walking-distance good" / "Right now, on your street").
- *Command:* /impeccable clarify app/index.tsx.

**[P2] Hierarchy buries the best line under the worst.**
- *What:* The atmospheric proof line is small slate-400 at the bottom; the urgency tagline is text-6xl font-black at the top.
- *Why it matters:* Squint test inverts the brand. Loud copy is the misaligned copy.
- *Fix:* Promote the proof line (or similar friend-voice line) to display scale. Demote or delete the urgency tagline.
- *Command:* /impeccable typeset app/index.tsx (or fold into bolder).

**[P3] Restraint without distinctive reference (second-order reflex).**
- *What:* Dark slate + one warm accent + medium sans + functional CTAs is the default 2026 consumer-marketplace lane.
- *Why it matters:* Brand register doc: restraint without intent reads as mediocre. The chassis could be any deals app.
- *Fix:* Name a real-world reference outside the consumer-app lane (hospitality-magazine warm cream + serif; neighborhood-bulletin paper; vending-machine industrial sans; late-night-diner menuboard).
- *Command:* /impeccable shape landing-voice to lock the reference before another visual rerun.

### Persona Red Flags

**Sam (Discovery-Seeker, primary persona from PRODUCT.md):** Sees amber on *gone.* and a tagline ending in *gone.* Reads as pressure. Misses the proof line. Bounce risk: medium-high.

**Maya (Groupon-Skeptic, anti-audience):** Pattern-matches "before they're gone" to dark-pattern coupon sites. Bounces before reading anything else. Bounce risk: high.

**Jordan (First-Timer, default persona):** Reads tagline, wonders which deals, which neighborhood. Proof line answers but is too small. Tap-through likely, informed tap-through less so. Risk: medium.

### Minor Observations

- tracking-[0.3em] on the DIBS kicker is editorial-magazine convention; "playful, casual, alive" might not want that calm-considered fingerprint.
- The "before they're / gone." line break orphans "gone." dramatically; intensifies the urgency reading even before the color fix.
- The accessibilityRole="header" placement is correct (one screen heading on the tagline). Don't add another.
